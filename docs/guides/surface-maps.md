The `realistic` and `painted` looks use the same surface maps. Each row describes the full-disc map for one body. Coverage describes the images used for the map. A global mosaic can contain gaps or images taken at different resolutions. Percentages refer to surface area unless the row says map height or flat map.

## Where the detail comes from

Sharp photographed terrain supplies the visible features. In blurred areas of the newer moon maps, the broad light and dark pattern and colour from the spacecraft images are kept where available. Fine detail there, and terrain in gaps where there are no images, are painted with an image model to continue the photographed ground, and are not observed. Some gaps instead fade to a plain tone based on nearby ground. The rows below say where each treatment was used.

## How close zoom loads a map

Most bodies bundle 512- and 1024-pixel-wide WebPs in `dist/textures/`. Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania and Oberon bundle the 512-pixel map. The default loader fetches their 1024-pixel maps and the 2048-pixel maps for close zoom from the matching GitHub release tag via jsDelivr.

A page that sets `texture-base` must serve the sizes it lets readers zoom to. Put wider WebPs in that folder or give them their own folder with `texture-far-base`. To offer finer tiles on a self-hosted page, give their folder with `tiles`. See [Close zoom and map tiles](close-zoom.md) and [Serve it yourself](serve-it-yourself.md).
