Pick a body. If it has moons, they come up on the row below it. Only the one in the big view moves; the others are still pictures.

To scale

Every body at the same scale, so the sizes can be compared. A body too small to see has a magnified picture beside it, joined by a line. The Sun is far larger than the page: only the edge of it fits.

Put it in your page

Set up the body above (which body, its look, the sky and date, the moons, turning), choose a size, then pick how your page is made and copy the code. The picture beside the code is made from the same choices.

Sizes, tilt and rotation: the IAU and NASA fact sheets. Positions: published theories (VSOP87B, ELP2000-82B and others) and fits to JPL Horizons. The realistic look is drawn from these numbers, with no pictures; the painted look uses NASA, USGS and JPL maps and a few CC BY 4.0 maps. Details in NOTICE.md
