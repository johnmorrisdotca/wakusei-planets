To scale

Every body at the same scale, so the sizes can be compared. A body too small to see has a magnified picture beside it, joined by a line. The Sun is far larger than the page: only the edge of it fits.

Put it in your page

Set up the body above (which body, its look, the sky and date, the moons, turning), choose a size, then pick how your page is made and copy the code. The picture beside the code is made from the same choices.
