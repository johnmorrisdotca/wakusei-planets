The moons of Jupiter

Every moon the catalogue gives Jupiter. A larger circle is a larger moon, and a higher circle is farther from Jupiter. The circles are drawn larger than true so a small moon can be seen. Colour is the moon's family.

Each outer group has its own rows, the farthest at the top. The gap between the inner moons and the outer groups is shortened, and neither that spacing nor Jupiter is to scale.
