# apollo-lrv

- Title: Mondfahrzeug (Lunar Rover)
- Author: Deutsches Museum | Digital (https://sketchfab.com/deutsches-museum)
- Licence: CC-BY-SA-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/mondfahrzeug-lunar-rover-acb3f8645a69493cb211a62b40d66aab

Modified: the model was turned and scaled, trimmed (about 440,000 vertices reduced to about 74,000), the four wheels were split out into their own nodes inside steering pivots, two eye points were added, the textures were shrunk and converted to WebP, and the file was packed with meshopt. `asset.extras` in the GLB carries the author, licence and source above, unchanged.

This file, `apollo-lrv.glb`, is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 as a whole, because it is an adaptation of a CC BY-SA 4.0 work. Anything that adapts it must be shared under the same licence.

The only other model considered, "Lunar Rover Toy/Model" by Cameron-Main (CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lunar-rover-toymodel-eae9a5b20d324fe6855fd3c4b37f41b6), was not used: see README.md.
